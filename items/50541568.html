Show HN: Tinnitus frequency finder and audio notch tool (trynotched.com)
197 points by brianrhea 3 days ago | 122 comments | 77.9% bullshit
I've had tinnitus for about a year and a half. There's no cure, so I'm trying anything that looks promising. One idea people are researching is listening to sound with a notch cut out right at your tinnitus frequency.

The frequency finder will help you zero in on your tinnitus pitch. The method write-up is here: https://trynotched.com/how-pitch-matching-works

Once you have a pitch, there's a free web app that plays background sounds (stream, rain, wind and so on) with a notch at your frequency: https://trynotched.com/tools.

I built it as a PWA so you can save to your phone's home screen and it works offline. No account or time limit.
